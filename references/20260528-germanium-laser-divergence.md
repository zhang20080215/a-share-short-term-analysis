## Divergence Analysis

### Factor 1: Concept Purity (primary driver)
- 云南锗业 is a **pure play** on germanium/small metals — any policy/catalyst about germanium directly hits it
- 华工科技 participates in semiconductor/optics sector — germanium is a minor input material, not core to its thesis

### Factor 2: Market Cap & Elasticity
- 云南锗业 ~300亿, small cap → easy to 涨停 with concentrated buying
- 华工科技 ~800亿+, large cap → +3.84% represents significant capital inflow already

### Factor 3: Prior Correction Depth
- 云南锗业: from 高105.17 to 低79.45 = **-24%** correction → high bounce elasticity
- 华工科技: from 高175.80 to 低155.71 = **-11%** correction → less oversold

### Factor 4: Supply/Resistance
- 云南锗业: After 10 consecutive down days, selling pressure exhausted → low resistance to涨停
- 华工科技: At 167, approaching 5/25-5/26 resistance zone (170-175 with套牢盘)

## Lesson for future
When user sees divergent performance in related-but-different-sector stocks, don't just report the difference — **decompose by concept purity first**. The 80% of variance is explained by how directly the catalyst hits each stock's core thesis.
